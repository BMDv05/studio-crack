Privacy notice

How we use booking data.

  • Why: to review your meeting request and reply by email.
  • What: only the fields on the booking form; phone and company are optional.
  • Who: the studio inbox (STUDIO_EMAIL). We never sell personal data.
  • Consent: the required checkbox records the time of consent (consent_at).
  • Retention: unconfirmed requests are deleted after 12 months; confirmed client records are kept only as long as needed for the relationship.
  • Your rights: ask for correction or deletion by emailing the studio with your reference number.
  • Analytics: none by default; any future analytics will be privacy-friendly and cookie-light.

Note: applicable privacy law depends on where the studio and clients are located. Have this notice reviewed against the rules that apply to the studio (spec 17).
